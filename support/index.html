FastRight support

FastRight works locally on your iPhone. These steps solve the most common issues without sharing your fasting data.

A reminder did not appear

Open iOS Settings, choose Notifications, then FastRight, and allow notifications. The daily start reminder pauses while a fast or break is active. Open FastRight after changing notification settings so it can update the schedule.

The widget or Live Activity is out of date

Open FastRight once to refresh its shared timer state. If needed, remove and add the widget again. iOS controls widget refresh timing. Live Activities also need to be enabled for FastRight in iOS Settings.

Move data to another iPhone

In FastRight Settings, create a JSON backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On the other iPhone, choose Restore backup. Review the confirmation carefully: restoring replaces the tracking data currently in the app.

Delete your data

Delete individual records or clear fasting history and water entries in the app. You can also delete the recovery copy from Settings. Removing FastRight deletes its remaining on-device data, but does not delete backup or export files you saved elsewhere.

Still stuck?

Use the developer contact shown on FastRight’s App Store listing and include your iOS version, FastRight version, and what you expected to happen. Do not include private fasting notes or backup files.
