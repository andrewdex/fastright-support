Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026. FastRight is designed to keep your fasting information private and on your device.

Information handling

FastRight stores fasting sessions, breaks, custom plans, reminder state, optional reflections and notes, optional water entries, and summary statistics locally on your device. It does not transmit this information to the developer or a remote server. FastRight has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party login, or cloud backend.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FastRight can schedule local notifications for the end of a fasting goal and an optional daily reminder to start a fast at the time you choose. The daily reminder pauses while a fast or break is active. FastRight does not use remote push notifications for marketing or data collection.

Export and backup

CSV exports and JSON backups are created locally and leave FastRight only when you choose a destination in the iOS share or Files interface. Backups are readable JSON and are not password-protected by FastRight. Restoring requires confirmation and keeps one local recovery copy, which you can save or delete in Settings. Files stored with another service are subject to that service’s policies.

Health information

FastRight does not access Apple Health or HealthKit. It is a timer and personal record, not a medical service.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Normal app updates retain existing records. Deleting app records does not delete files you previously exported; delete those from the destination where you saved them.

Children

FastRight is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If FastRight’s data practices change, this policy and its App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released. For privacy questions, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so do not include fasting records, backup files, or personal health information.
